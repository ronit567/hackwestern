Operating room early warning

Every 30 s during surgery, the model estimates whether hypotension (arterial MAP below 65 mmHg for 60 s) or hypoxemia (SpO2 below 90 % for 2 min) starts in the next 5, 10 or 15 minutes. The claim under test: it warns earlier and more accurately than watching the current number, including while that number still looks normal.
